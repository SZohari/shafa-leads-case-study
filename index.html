01 / THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

The visit ends.
The information shouldn't.

A field-sales team needs more than a customer list. It needs to know what happened, what comes next and which information can be trusted — even when the connection drops.

Shafa Leads brings customer records, daily plans, visits, phone calls and follow-ups into a single system. Managers can work from structured activity and sales data, while field staff have a practical workflow on their phones.

02 / WHERE THE INTERESTING WORK HAPPENED

Built around
real working conditions.

The valuable part is the thinking between the screens: what happens when the network fails, a location is uncertain or a follow-up is missed?

01⇄

Connection lost.
Work preserved.

Drafts and pending reports remain on the device until a server receipt confirms submission. Account-scoped queues and duplicate protection support safe retries.

BUSINESS VALUE Continuity for the field team
02◎

Visit evidence.
With honest uncertainty.

Visit tracking separates elapsed time from estimated presence using location samples. Missing or unreliable GPS remains unknown and can be flagged for review.

BUSINESS VALUE More interpretable field reporting
03↗

A next step.
Beyond the first sale.

An 11-stage sales journey connects qualification, demos, negotiation and repeat purchases. Native Android reminders support follow-ups after the app is closed, when permissions are enabled.

BUSINESS VALUE Clear ownership and follow-through
04▥

Field activity.
Management-ready data.

Structured customer imports, activity filters, sales charts and multi-sheet Excel exports connect daily operations to review and analysis. Historical sales stages stay meaningful as records evolve.

BUSINESS VALUE A consistent basis for decisions

04 / PROCESS BEFORE FEATURES

A sales journey.
From first contact to repeat business.

The workflow follows the team's actual sales process, from the first record through repeat business. Explore the three phases.

Document sharing, quotations and collections are represented as workflow stages, notes and follow-ups; they are not standalone document, invoicing or payment-processing modules.

05 / BEYOND THE INTERFACE

Designed for use.
Checked for the difficult moments.

My focus was connecting the business process, the data model and the user's daily work — and carrying those decisions through implementation and validation.

That includes role-scoped access, traceable customer edits, real-device Android checks, deployment environments and recovery procedures. AI supported development; checking behavior and understanding its limits remained part of the work.
