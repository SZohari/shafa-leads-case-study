## The product in operation

- **Field representatives:** daily plans, customers, visits, calls, outcomes and follow-ups.
- **Supervisors:** team planning and GPS-supported visit review, including location evidence, timing, explanations and review decisions.
- **Managers:** role-scoped activity views, filters, charts and multi-sheet Excel exports.
- **Connected sales work:** eleven stages, traceable customer changes and Android reminders with the required permissions enabled.
- **Continuity:** account-scoped drafts and pending reports, server receipts and duplicate protection support interrupted work.

GPS monitoring is tied to a customer visit. Elapsed time, estimated presence and unknown intervals remain distinct. Navigation opens an external map application. Live fleet mapping and automatic route optimization are not represented as implemented capabilities.

## Explore the four-stage product tour

The overview leads with **English presentation adaptations based on real test-environment screens**, using illustrative sample data. Its English/German tour explains a connected workflow:

1. **Review a visit:** inspect sample GPS evidence and compare evidence-available and needs-review conditions.
2. **Record outcome:** choose an outcome and save a visit note.
3. **Plan follow-up:** choose the next action and save its date.
4. **Review activity:** see the saved outcome and task together; return to edit either.

The tour uses explanatory interfaces and sample records. Its GPS graphic is a conceptual illustration, not an in-app map screenshot. Tour choices stay in page memory; reset clears them. The tour does not access customer records or device GPS. The operational application has its own authenticated Persian RTL interface.

## My contribution

My work connected business-process design, data logic, AI-assisted implementation, validation and delivery. It included role-scoped access, traceable changes, GPS review logic, recovery from interrupted submissions and real-device Android checks.

The operational stack includes **Next.js, TypeScript, SQLite, Android/Java, ExcelJS and Linux/Caddy**. Document sharing, quotations and collection follow-up are workflow stages, notes and next actions; they are not standalone document, invoicing or payment modules.
